Pick a scale/key to auto-check matching notes, then scrub. The drone chord has its own key, so you can scrub a scale over a chord in a different key on purpose. The progression follows the scale's Key. Flip on Enable Beat for basic drums under the progression. Slur glides pitch continuously between notes instead of jumping (turns off Sustain, since they conflict). Quantize snaps normal scrubbed notes onto a rhythmic grid — set by Note Length and the Tempo field — instead of firing exactly when you cross a key. Reverse Envelope swells each keyboard note up to full volume and cuts it off, instead of the normal pluck-and-decay. Delay only affects the keyboard channel.
